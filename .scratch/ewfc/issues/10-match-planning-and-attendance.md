# Match planning and attendance

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 04, 06

## Question

How does the next match get planned, and how does intent become attendance?

Pin down: what an admin sets when planning (place, date/time, booked hours, expected cost, deposit nudge). Do players RSVP in-app, and are RSVP states just yes/no/maybe or does the app cap the roster? How does RSVP become actual attendance — does the admin check people in on the night? Who is billable: everyone who attended, or everyone who RSVP'd yes? How does the attendance list hand off to the team randomizer? Are recurring matches a thing (every Sunday 20:00) or is each one planned fresh?
