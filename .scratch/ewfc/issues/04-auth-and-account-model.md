# Auth and account model

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

How do accounts get created, secured, and recovered — given no public signup?

Pin down: what identifies a player at login (phone number, a handle, something else)? What does the admin hand over — an initial password, a one-time code, a link? Must the player change it, and what happens if they never do? What roles exist (admin, player, anything between — referee? treasurer?) and what can each see and do? How does a player who forgot their password get back in with no email on file? Can a Person exist in the ledger with no account at all (a guest who played once)?
