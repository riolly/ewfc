# Team randomizer rules

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Given N attendees and a chosen format, how does the app produce teams?

Pin down: how head count maps to team count and team size (and what happens on an awkward count like 13). How keepers are spread — is "keeper" a flag on a Person, a per-session declaration, or a per-game role? What the admin can do after the shuffle: swap two players, lock a player to a team, re-roll. Is a shuffle reproducible/undoable, or is each roll final once accepted?

Explicitly out of this ticket: skill ratings (out of scope) and never-played-together pairing history (fog — revisit later).
