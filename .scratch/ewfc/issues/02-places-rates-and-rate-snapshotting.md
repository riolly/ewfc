# Places, rates and rate snapshotting

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

What is a Place, and how does its rate reach a match's bill?

Pin down: fields on Place (name, Google Maps location — link, coordinates, or place id?), rate per hour, and whether one place has several rates (peak/off-peak, weekday/weekend, court number). When a place's rate changes, past matches must not silently re-price — so is the rate snapshotted onto the match at booking time? Are there per-booking extras (ball rental, lights, deposit to venue) that belong on the bill but not in the hourly rate?
