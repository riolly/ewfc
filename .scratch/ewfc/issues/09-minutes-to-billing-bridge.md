# Minutes-to-billing bridge

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 06, 07

## Question

How do console events become the numbers the split formula consumes?

Pin down: how sub events fold into per-player minutes, and what counts as on-court time (is a player benched by rotation accruing minutes? settled: no, but confirm against the floor). How the keeper weight is derived from console data. What the system does when console data is missing or wrong — no console used at all, someone forgot to sub a player off, the session ended mid-game: is there a manual minutes override per player, and does overriding leave a visible mark?

Deliverable: the contract between the match-day model and the ledger, including the degraded path.
