# Futsal rotation formats

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: —

## Question

What rotation systems do amateur futsal communities actually use for 8–20 players, and how does each behave?

Cover at minimum: winner-stays (king of the court), fixed rotation blocks, round-robin across 3+ teams, and two-fixed-teams-with-rolling-subs. For each: how many teams and what size, how a "game" is bounded (time, first-to-N goals), how many games fit a 2-hour booking, how court time distributes across players, and how it degrades when the head count is odd or someone leaves early.

Deliverable: a comparison the app can turn into a format picker keyed on head count — the community currently burns time deciding this on the spot.
